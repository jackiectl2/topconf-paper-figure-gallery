# Step 4 original bitmap export

When a FigureForge Step 3 bitmap draft is continued into Step 4, every confirmed Step 4 export ZIP must contain the editable output, the original Step 3 bitmap, and `references.json`. The bitmap keeps its actual PNG or JPEG format and the Step 3 draft identifier. The manifest identifies its filename. SVG-first drafts and standalone Step 4 uploads have no Step 3 bitmap to attach. No generation or reconstruction behavior changes.

Develop on a new branch and leave merging, pushing, and App packaging for a later owner decision.
